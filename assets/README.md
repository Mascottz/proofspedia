# Proofspedia brand kit

The mark is a drawn P with a ruled baseline and terminal point, an editorial monogram for mathematical proof and printed folios. Its palette and spacing follow the site's warm, restrained design.

## Marks

- `proofspedia-mark.svg` is the standalone logo for light backgrounds.
- `proofspedia-mark-reversed.svg` is the same logo for dark backgrounds.
- `proofspedia-lockup.svg` is the horizontal wordmark for cover pages and documents.

Keep clear space around each mark. Do not redraw, stretch, or add effects to the SVGs.

## Icons and sharing

`favicon.svg` and `favicon.ico` are browser tab icons. The 32 pixel PNG, 180 pixel Apple touch icon, and 192 and 512 pixel app icons are linked by the site and web manifest. `pinned-tab.svg` is the monochrome Safari icon. The social preview is a 1200 by 630 PNG with an editable SVG source, used by the Open Graph and Twitter metadata.
