What makes a proof belong here

Elegance is not prettiness alone. It is inevitability ; the argument that leaves no alternative, that uses exactly what is needed and no more, that could be explained to a thoughtful peer in minutes and remembered for years.

This archive collects the most luminous proof for each theorem we cover. Every folio keeps its originator, its statement, its proof in steps, its intuition, and why it stays. All proofs read on the page ; no external link is the source.

It is continually updated ; new folios are set, existing ones can be refined, and all changes are recorded in the ledger with credit.

No en dash or em dash ; only ; , → and hyphenated words

How a folio is chosen

  • Brevity ; the shorter road is preferred when it loses nothing 01
  • Surprise ; the method could not be guessed from the statement 02
  • Generality ; the idea illuminates beyond one theorem 03
  • Clarity ; a second-year student can follow, a researcher can admire 04

To propose ; open a discussion with statement, source, and a 5-line sketch of why it is elegant. Submissions are rendered as folios before review, with proposer credited as contributor.
